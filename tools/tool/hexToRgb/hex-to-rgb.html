how to convat (hex to rgb/rgba)

Example Hex Code: #4CAF50

  • Hex Code: #4CAF50

    The hex code is broken down into three pairs:

    • Red: 4C
    • Green: AF
    • Blue: 50

  • Convert Each Pair from Hex to Decimal:

    Red: 4C in hexadecimal → Convert to decimal:

    • 4C = 4 × 16 + 12 = 76
    • Red = 76

    Green: AF in hexadecimal → Convert to decimal:

    • AF = 10 × 16 + 15 = 175
    • Green = 175

    Blue: 50 in hexadecimal → Convert to decimal:

    • 50 = 5 × 16 + 0 = 80
    • Blue = 80

  • Resulting RGB Value:

    The RGB equivalent of #4CAF50 is: rgb(76, 175, 80)

  • Resulting RGBA Value:

    The RGBA equivalent of #4CAF50 with 50% opacity is: rgba(76, 175, 80, 0.5)
